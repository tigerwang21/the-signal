AI in Teaching & Learning

02

Manchester's four-category assessment policy takes effect

Every assessment is tagged AI-Prohibited / -Minimal / -Permitted / -Integrated, stated on the brief, with only the university Copilot able to touch student-identifiable data. A ready-to-adapt taxonomy that operationalises AI policy into everyday practice.

94% of UK undergraduates use AI in assessed work

HEPI's survey of 1,054 students (reported in Nature) makes near-universal AI use an empirical fact, with 12% inserting AI text directly. The question has moved from "permit AI?" to "how do we assess learning when AI is ubiquitous?" — redesign, not detection.
