Surrey embeds discipline-specific AI across every degree

AI in Teaching & Learning

02

HEPI: 95% of undergraduates use AI, but support lags sharply

The third Student Generative AI Survey finds 95% use AI and 94% in assessed work — yet only 36% feel encouraged and 38% are given tools. Closing that provision gap is now the highest-ROI teaching intervention.

descriptionSOURCE: HEPI ↗

Frontier AI cannot yet grade essays reliably

Across 761 psychology essays, AI matched human degree classifications only 35–65% of the time, rewarding length and vocabulary over judgement — a strong UK evidence base to resist automating marking.

descriptionSOURCE: PHYS.ORG ↗

Most AI policies are compliance instruments in educational language

HEPI's review of 96 institutions finds many "promise critical thinking but deliver surveillance." Policy quality is now a differentiator — audit whether yours builds critical literacy or defaults to detection.

descriptionSOURCE: HEPI / ACADEMICJOBS ↗

China mandates AI literacy as a public required course

The "AI + Education" Action Plan makes AI a compulsory foundational course at every university — 626 institutions now run AI majors and over 600,000 students are enrolled. A whole-system benchmark, though one to read alongside China's very different governance model.

AI Workforce & Skills

03

UK graduate vacancies hit a decade low as AI automates entry-level work

Adzuna records just 8,383 graduate jobs in July — down 45% year-on-year — against a record 262,820 students entering this September. Graduate employability is now a structural AI risk.

descriptionSOURCE: THE GUARDIAN / ADZUNA ↗

Government scales free AI training to 10 million workers by 2030

The DfE-led AI Skills Boost extends free AI training across the workforce. A national upskilling push universities can anchor to through CPD, degree apprenticeships and employer partnerships.

descriptionSOURCE: GOV.UK ↗

Faculty capability, not tools, is the binding constraint

Pearson/AWS finds only 13% of higher-ed leaders rate faculty AI knowledge "very strong." The highest-leverage investment is faculty upskilling, not tool procurement.

US: the skills mismatch costs $1.1 trillion a year

AI Policy & Operations

04

DSIT's call for evidence on data regulation closes 9 September

It seeks practical examples of where UK GDPR and the Data (Use and Access) Act break down under AI. A rare, imminent window to shape UK data law — respond now with examples from research, admissions or student analytics.

descriptionSOURCE: GOV.UK ↗

The Data (Use and Access) Act's data-protection provisions are now in force

Since 5 February, the new "recognised legitimate interest" basis, reworked DSAR rules, and a new deepfake offence apply. Refresh RoPAs, DPIAs and DSAR procedures — the ICO has flagged universities as particularly affected.

descriptionSOURCE: DLA PIPER ↗

DSIT is being dismantled and redistributed across Whitehall

Science and innovation move to a new department, digital regulation returns to DCMS, and a Cabinet-level AI minister oversees a new Cabinet Office AI Taskforce. Recalibrate which Whitehall interlocutors you engage.
